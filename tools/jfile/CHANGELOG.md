# jfile changelog

## 0.2.0
* **Added:** the places are a list of your own: `n` in the places makes a new folder in `u:/` and keeps it as a place, `+` in a folder keeps that folder as a place, `d` takes one of yours away (`~/.jfile_places`); a standard folder that is not there yet (Documents, Pictures, ...) is made when you ask; a Desktop place.
* **Fixed:** none.

## 0.1.0
* **Added:** the screen of places and folders (look, open, copy, cut, paste, delete, rename, new folder, find, sort, hidden files, marks), the viewers of pictures (png, jpg) and text, playing a video or a song by the app, `o` to open a file with the app of the phone; the commands `ls`, `places`, `cp`, `mv`, `rm`, `mkdir` for scripts.
* **Fixed:** none (first release).
