# jfile changelog

## 0.1.0
* **Added:** the screen of places and folders (look, open, copy, cut, paste, delete, rename, new folder, find, sort, hidden files, marks), the viewers of pictures (png, jpg) and text, playing a video or a song by the app, `o` to open a file with the app of the phone; the commands `ls`, `places`, `cp`, `mv`, `rm`, `mkdir` for scripts.
* **Fixed:** none (first release).
