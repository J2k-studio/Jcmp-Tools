# Changelog

## 0.2.0
* **Added:** in the Jaric app (0.0.208+) `mulap` opens the studio, a window in landscape: 7 pads (kick, snare, hat, openhat, clap, tom, bass) that play when touched, a step grid of 16 steps for every voice (touch or drag), play and stop with the steps lit, tempo, swing, a starting beat, clear, save and load as a plan in `u:/Music/mulap/NAME.mlp` that `mulap render` turns into a WAV.
* **Fixed:** none.

## 0.1.0
* **Added:** the engine: `mulap render PLAN.mlp OUT.wav` and `mulap demo OUT.wav`; a plan has bpm, bars, swing and tracks of 16-step patterns; the voices kick, snare, hat, openhat, clap, tom and bass are made by the program; a beat is the same every time it is rendered.
* **Fixed:** none (first version).
